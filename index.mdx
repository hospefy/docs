HostDear helps accommodation owners describe what guests can book, publish that information, and receive enquiries.

You can use it for a single holiday home, several accommodations at one location, or a portfolio of properties. You do not need to configure every feature before you begin.

## Start with your real accommodation

Choose the guide that sounds most like your business. The guide will introduce each HostDear concept when you need it.
